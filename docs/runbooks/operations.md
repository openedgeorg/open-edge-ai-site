# Operations

## What is live in this tree

Ordinary unit, contract, and integration tests run against the local PostgreSQL, Valkey, and LocalStack lab. They do not qualify a provider.

`scripts/qualify.py` commands `g1`, `g2`, `capacity`, `da01`, `privacy`, and `release` stay pending or blocked without recorded evidence. `scripts/evaluate.py section23` and `g3` do the same. Do not read provider credentials to turn those into a pass.

## Outages and uncertainty

A lost acceptance response is reconciled with `GET /conversations/{id}/status`. The client does not post the draft again. Cancellation and recovery do not replay visitor text. A missing storage fence blocks replacement of a native writer.

## Drain and rollback

Drain only unloads warm-idle sessions that are not in the middle of a history read. Existing conversation pins stay on their original Brain and behavior. Rollback of a package does not rewrite those pins and does not edit native files.

## Audit

Telemetry must not record visitor text. A verified dispatch can proceed when the audit sink fails after the evidence row is stored. An unobserved dispatch blocks release readiness.
